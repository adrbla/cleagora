# General Context – CLEAgora

This document synthesizes the key concepts from h2\'s ecosystem that CLEAgora developers and AI assistants need to understand. Source documents are provided separately, not in this repo.

## h2\ — the agency

h2\ is an augmented marketing intelligence agency. Core disciplines: qualitative research, quantitative studies, semiotics, strategic consulting. The team has deep expertise in social psychology, semiology, and cognitive science.

The agency's thesis: generative AI doesn't replace expertise — it amplifies it. The challenge is methodological, not technological. h2\ has built an integrated system (DECODia) to operationalize this principle.

## DECODia — the system

DECODia is h2\'s augmented intelligence system. Three inseparable layers:

1. **Technical capabilities**: sovereign infrastructure (private deployment, no third-party SaaS), corpus ingestion (text, audio, video, image), systematic metadata generation, versioned pipelines, advanced document search.

2. **Orchestration method**: context construction (intentional structuring of sources), query engineering (calibrated prompts aligned with analytical intent), calibrated assistants (specialized agents for specific tasks), continuous expert supervision.

3. **Operational logic — "intrication"**: human and model work together at every step. Systematic (every step is designed and controlled) but never automatic (nothing runs without intention and supervision). The human conceives, interprets, decides. The AI explores, structures, amplifies.

## CLEA — the qualitative module

CLEA (Consumer-Level Elicitation Architecture) is DECODia's conversational qualitative research module. Core sequence: **structured → unstructured → restructured**.

1. Start with an interview guide (sections, questions, stimuli, probing logic).
2. Transform it into a natural conversation via a calibrated AI assistant. The assistant adapts tone, rhythm, and follow-ups based on what the participant expresses. Multimodal: text, voice, image, video.
3. Automatically restructure the collected material. Each unit of meaning (verbatim) is placed back into the original structure and enriched with metadata: descriptive (entities, places, products, emotions, tones) and analytical (linked to study questions).

**Three actors**: the participant (expresses freely), the conversational assistant (animates, probes, deepens), the researcher (designs, supervises, interprets).

**Key principles**: Consumer In The Loop (authentic human voice at the center), Human In The Loop (researcher in control of interpretation).

**Scale**: 300–500 respondents per study. Qualitative depth at quantitative scale.

## CLEAgora — the community variant

CLEAgora adapts CLEA for collective dynamics. Instead of 1:1 conversations, respondents interact with each other on a Discourse-based forum. The value shift:

- **From individual depth to collective intelligence**: debates, co-construction, mutual stimulation between participants.
- **From sequential to organic**: discussions unfold naturally, with emergent dynamics that a 1:1 format cannot capture.
- **Same rigor downstream**: the extraction and enrichment pipeline maintains CLEA's structured approach to metadata generation and analysis-ready output.

This positions CLEAgora alongside online qualitative communities and bulletin board studies, but with DECODia's systematic enrichment capabilities.
